# Wauld

1. Log in to your Wauld account.
2. Open `Integrations` from the left navigation bar.
3. Select `Automatisch` from the integration gallery.
4. Click `Generate Token`.
5. Copy the generated access token.
6. Find your Wauld Account ID in the URL after `wauld.com/`. It starts with `acc_`. Copy the Account ID.
7. In Automatisch, open `My Apps` and click `Add connection`.
8. Select `Wauld`.
9. Paste your Wauld access token into the `Access Token` field.
10. Paste your Wauld Account ID into the `Account ID` field.
11. Click `Save`.
12. Start using the Wauld integration with Automatisch.
